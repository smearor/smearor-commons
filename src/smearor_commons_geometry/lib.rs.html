1//! Generic 2D geometry primitives for the Smearor ecosystem.
2//!
3//! Pure mathematics without framework dependencies: unit-tagged vectors,
4//! rotations, rectangles and shapes. `serde` is unconditional;
5//! `async-graphql`/`schemars` scalar and schema derives and conversions
6//! to `gtk4`, `cairo` and `smithay` types live behind optional feature
7//! gates.
8//!
9//! The unit newtypes ([`Millimeters`], [`Pixels`],
10//! [`SquareMillimeters`], [`PixelsPerMm`], [`UnitsPerMm`]) keep raw
11//! `f64` values unconfusable and double as the `Vec2<U>` unit markers.
12//!
13//! One model serves both domain math and framework boundaries:
14//! [`Vec2`]`<U, T>` and [`Rect`]`<U, T>` are generic over the
15//! coordinate space `U` *and* the numeric component type `T` (default
16//! `f64`). `Vec2<Millimeters>` is a tagged domain point;
17//! `Vec2<(), i32>` is the *unit-erased* boundary form that converts
18//! into smithay `Point<i32, K>` of any space kind `K`, graphene `f32`
19//! primitives or `i32` texture extents. [`Position`], [`Size`] and
20//! [`Rectangle`] are readable aliases for these erased forms -
21//! the same type, not parallel representations.
