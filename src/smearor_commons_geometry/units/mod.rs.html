1//! Scalar unit types used by the geometry primitives.
2//!
3//! The types in this module are unit-tagged `f64` newtypes, giving
4//! dimensional safety at zero runtime cost: `Millimeters` cannot be mixed up
5//! with `Pixels`, `Degrees` not with `Radians`, and `PixelsPerMm`/`UnitsPerMm`
6//! convert between device and physical coordinates. The coordinate-space
7//! units double as `Vec2<U>` unit markers; the angular units
8//! ([`Degrees`], [`Radians`]) are plain scalar boundaries for the
9//! quantity type [`crate::RotationRad`].
10
