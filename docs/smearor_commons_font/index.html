Font metric utilities on [ab_glyph] fonts.

Glyph scale fitting (glyph_fit_scale), bounds centering (centered_glyph) and width truncation (truncate_text_to_width), expressed in smearor_commons_geometry units ([Pixels], [Vec2], [Rect]). These are the pure computations — font loading and caching policy stay with the consumer.

Functions§

centered_glyph
A glyph scaled to scale and positioned so that the center of its pixel bounds lands on target — outline px_bounds when available (precise for centering), glyph_bounds as fallback.
glyph_fit_scale
The em-scale that makes the glyph’s largest pixel dimension equal target_px.
truncate_text_to_width
Truncates text to fit within max_width at the scaled font.
