Expand description

Progress curve of an animation: maps linear time progress (0.0..=1.0) to eased progress. Eased values may exceed the range (e.g. Overshoot) - trajectories decide whether extrapolation is meaningful for their value type.

Variants§

§

Linear

Constant speed - eased progress equals time progress.

§

EaseIn

Slow start, fast finish (quadratic ease-in).

§

EaseOut

Fast start, slow finish (quadratic ease-out).

§

EaseInOut

Slow start and end, fast middle (quadratic ease-in-out).

§

EaseInCubic

Slow start, fast finish (cubic ease-in).

§

EaseOutCubic

Fast start, slow finish (cubic ease-out) - the classic “arrive” curve for camera and pose transitions.

§

EaseInOutCubic

Slow start and end, fast middle (cubic ease-in-out).

§

Overshoot

Goes below 0, then beyond the target and settles back (Penner’s back-in-out).

Fields

§overshoot_amount: f64

Overshoot strength - higher values overshoot further.

§

EaseInQuart

Slow start, fast finish (quartic ease-in, t^4).

§

EaseOutQuart

Fast start, slow finish (quartic ease-out).

§

EaseInOutQuart

Slow start and end, fast middle (quartic ease-in-out).

§

EaseInQuint

Slow start, fast finish (quintic ease-in, t^5).

§

EaseOutQuint

Fast start, slow finish (quintic ease-out).

§

EaseInOutQuint

Slow start and end, fast middle (quintic ease-in-out).

§

EaseInSine

Slow start, fast finish (sinusoidal ease-in).

§

EaseOutSine

Fast start, slow finish (sinusoidal ease-out) - the gentlest curve of the family.

§

EaseInOutSine

Slow start and end, fast middle (sinusoidal ease-in-out).

§

EaseInExpo

Very slow start, abrupt finish (exponential ease-in).

§

EaseOutExpo

Abrupt start, very slow finish (exponential ease-out).

§

EaseInOutExpo

Abrupt middle between very slow ends (exponential ease-in-out).

§

EaseInCirc

Slow start, fast finish (circular ease-in).

§

EaseOutCirc

Fast start, slow finish (circular ease-out).

§

EaseInOutCirc

Slow start and end, fast middle (circular ease-in-out).

§

BackIn

Dips below the start value, then accelerates (Penner’s back-in).

Fields

§overshoot_amount: f64

Overshoot strength - higher values dip further.

§

BackOut

Overshoots the target, then settles back (Penner’s back-out). Self::Overshoot is the in-out combination of this and Self::BackIn.

Fields

§overshoot_amount: f64

Overshoot strength - higher values overshoot further.

§

Elastic

Oscillates around the target and settles - the springy ease-out of the easing literature.

Fields

§amplitude: f64

Oscillation amplitude - 1.0 is the classic curve.

§period: f64

Oscillation period in progress units - the classic curve uses 0.3.

§

Bounce

Bounces off the target with decreasing heights - the physical drop-in of the easing literature.

§

CubicBezier

A custom cubic bezier curve - CSS cubic-bezier() compatible: x1, y1 are the outgoing control point, x2, y2 the incoming one. y may exceed 0..1, x is clamped to it.

Fields

§x1: f64

X of the outgoing control point.

§y1: f64

Y of the outgoing control point.

§x2: f64

X of the incoming control point.

§y2: f64

Y of the incoming control point.

§

Steps

Discrete progress: steps equally sized jumps, CSS steps() compatible.

Fields

§steps: u32

The number of discrete steps.

§jump: StepsJump

When the jump happens within each step interval.
