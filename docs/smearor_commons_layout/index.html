Crate smearor_commons_layout 

Source
Expand description

Tabletop layout primitives - arrangement vocabulary without domain semantics.

TableSide names the four edges of a rectangular surface; a Placement is the technical target of an arrangement (center + rotation + footprint). A LayoutStrategy maps a list of LayoutItems onto Placements inside bounds - the built-ins are FlowLayout (side-by-side lines, non-overlapping) and SeatLayout (the four table seats). Consumer crates keep their semantic types (“player”, “card”, “note”) and map them onto these primitives - which side a user sits at, or what a placed item is, stays downstream.

Pure std with unconditional serde; all geometry is unit-generic over the U marker of Vec2/Rect from smearor-commons-geometry, so pixels, millimeters and erased coordinates share the same algorithms.

Line-flow arrangement: items side by side from the anchor corner until the far edge of bounds, then wrapping to the next line.
