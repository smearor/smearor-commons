fn glyph(&self, tile: Vec2<()>, uv: Vec2<()>) -> f64

Coverage of glyph tile at uv within one glyph cell, 0..1.

uv is the in-cell coordinate (0..1 each axis, y down); tile is the atlas tile index 0..256 (BMW’s 16x16 grid).
