GTK adapter - attaches the headless effect engine to GTK widgets.

Three pieces (phase 5):

  • snapshot - renders a widget into a frozen [PixelBuffer<Rgba>] texture once per animation.
  • FxPlayer - a DrawingArea subclass that owns the clock and buffers and plays any [EffectSpec].
  • SurfaceProvider - the callback layered (Layers::SurfaceAndWindow) effects pull their backdrop texture from.

The adapter never owns the real widget: hiding/showing it around the animation stays consumer policy. The fx.rs experimenter is the working reference for the same frame pipeline.
