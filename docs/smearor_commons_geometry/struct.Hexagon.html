Struct Hexagon 

Source 
pub struct Hexagon<U = (), T = f64> {
    pub center: Vec2<U, T>,
    pub circumradius: T,
    pub orientation: PolygonOrientation,
}
Expand description

A regular hexagon tagged with its coordinate unit U and numeric component type T.

A regular hexagon is fully determined by a single extent: the circumradius (center to vertex) equals its edge length, and the inradius (center to edge, also called apothem) is circumradius * sin(60°). Use Self::with_inradius or Self::with_side_length when the hexagon is specified by one of the other extents.

T defaults to f64 for domain math. The type is a technical primitive without validation: circumradius should be non-negative, stricter bounds belong to consumer wrappers.

Fields§

§center: Vec2<U, T>

The center point.

§circumradius: T

The circumradius - distance from center to each vertex. For a regular hexagon this equals the edge length.

§orientation: PolygonOrientation

Whether the hexagon has a horizontal top edge (PolygonOrientation::FlatTop) or a vertex on top (PolygonOrientation::PointyTop).

Whether point lies inside the hexagon - the boundary counts as inside.

A regular hexagon is the intersection of three strips perpendicular to its three edge normals: y (FlatTop) resp. x (PointyTop) and the two ±60° axes.

pub fn inscribed_square_side(&self) -> f64

Side of the largest centered axis-aligned square inscribed in the hexagon: (3 - sqrt(3)) * circumradius, its corners resting on the four slanted edges. The factor is the same for both orientations - a square turned a quarter turn is a square.
