Tabletop layout primitives - arrangement vocabulary without domain semantics.

TableSide names the four edges of a rectangular surface; a Placement is the technical target of an arrangement (center + quarter-turn rotation + footprint). Consumer crates keep their semantic types (“player”, “card”, “note”) and map them onto these primitives - which side a user sits at, or what a placed item is, stays downstream.

Pure std with unconditional serde; all geometry is unit-generic over the U marker of Vec2/Rect from smearor-commons-geometry, so pixels, millimeters and erased coordinates share the same algorithms.
