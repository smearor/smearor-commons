Expand description

Converts snapped four-orientation vocabularies into the layer-shell screen [Edge] they face.

QuarterTurn, TableSide and Rotation name the same four sides of a surface in different domains - reading rotation, seat position and layer-shell anchor. edge() maps them onto that single edge, replacing per-app match tables and float-epsilon comparisons of raw degree values.
