Timed animations for the Smearor ecosystem.

Pure std core: an TimedAnimation pairs a Trajectory - the shape of the animated output - with a [Duration] and an EasingFunction. Trajectories compose: Span interpolates between two values of any Interpolate type, ThreePhaseSpan eases out, holds and eases back, and (S1, S2) tuples animate composite values on one clock. The gtk4 feature gate adds the frame-clock driver that pumps an animation on a widget.

serde is unconditional; schemars schema derives live behind their feature gate.

Structs§

Span
A two-endpoint trajectory: interpolates from -> to along the progress t.
ThreePhaseSpan
A three-phase trajectory: interpolates from -> mid over the leading lead fraction of progress, holds mid until trail, then interpolates mid -> to over the remainder.
TimedAnimation
An eased, timed animation over any Trajectory: pairs the trajectory with a Duration and an EasingFunction and produces eased values once Self::started.

Enums§

EasingFunction
Progress curve of an animation: maps linear time progress (0.0..=1.0) to eased progress. Eased values may exceed the range (e.g. Overshoot) - trajectories decide whether extrapolation is meaningful for their value type.

Traits§

Interpolate
A value that can be interpolated between two endpoints along a normalized progress t (0 = self, 1 = target).
Trajectory
Maps eased progress t (conventionally 0.0..=1.0, easing may exceed it) to an animated value - the shape of an animation’s output over time.

Functions§

drive
Drives animation on widget’s frame clock: each frame calls apply with the eased value until the animation completes. apply returning [ControlFlow::Break] stops early.

A rotation-zoom composite - shortest-arc rotation plus a three-phase zoom (out, hold, back in) on one clock.
